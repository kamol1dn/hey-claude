Pick a companion for hey-claude

Five original characters. Each one is drawn live at 120 px as a desktop companion and at true size inside the island pill. Move your pointer near one and it notices you. "Show in island" puts it in the pill at the top of the screen.

All five

Notes

  • The mic level and the voice amplitude are simulated (speech-like syllables), and every card shares the same signal, so the five react to the same "sentence".
  • Calm motion follows prefers-reduced-motion: idle goes still, hops, particles and flights are dropped, and only information-bearing motion (mic and voice level, state changes) remains.
  • Everything is drawn with canvas and requestAnimationFrame. No images, fonts or network requests.
