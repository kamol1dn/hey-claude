How to read this

  • The ember is Claude's presence; its temperature, breath and glow tell the state. The thread is the voice line: white = you, terracotta = Claude.
  • Every morph is a spring that is only ever retargeted: click any state mid-transition (or run chaos) and the motion carries its velocity into the new shape. Growing springs overshoot slightly (inhale); shrinking ones never do (exhale).
  • The preview runs the same driver tiers as the QML plan: every vsync only while something moves, then a timer tick at rest (ready 8 fps, tasks 10, paused 2, error hold 4), and in off or calm idle the loop stops completely. The Preview readout counts real frames.
  • While Claude thinks or talks, the pill's right end carries two buttons: mic (interrupt and talk: barge-in from the current pose) and stop. Esc or a click on the speaking pill also stops it.
  • Pause is compressed: 1 hour plays in 12 s, and the pre-warm runs over the last 2 s.
