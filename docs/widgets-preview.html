Island
  • Morph: the pill's width and height are springs: growing "inhales" (≈4.5 % overshoot), shrinking "exhales" (none). Content enters once the panel is ~70 % open, staggered 45 ms per row, and leaves before it shrinks.
  • Header: the ember and the thread stay where they are in the pill; the service glyph slides in next to them; the title says what the card is.
  • Motion on update: numbers tick to their new value, bars grow from the left, unread badges pop, the timer's ember rides the ring.
  • Weather is animated in the top-right of the panel: sun with a slowly turning ring of dots (never rays), drifting clouds, falling rain, snow, a storm flash, fog bands, stars at night. It runs while the card is new, then settles to ~8 fps, and stops when the island rests.
  • Reduced motion: no springs or overshoot, 160 ms crossfades, static weather art, numbers set instantly.
  • Weather+ (M5b, weather-mcp): an official warning as a banner in its severity's colour (yellow, orange, red; severe ones breathe twice) with the issuing service credited; a "rain in N minutes" line with the next two hours as 15-minute bars that grow from the bottom; an AQI dot, UV and pollen on the left of a strip with sunrise, sunset and the moon's lit share on the right. Each is hidden when its data isn't there, and the days give up rows so the card stays within 280 px. The built-in Open-Meteo fallback still fills sun, UV and the moon (computed locally).
  • Data in this page is sample data. In the app, weather comes from weather-mcp (the local weather route) or the built-in Open-Meteo tool, never from the model.
